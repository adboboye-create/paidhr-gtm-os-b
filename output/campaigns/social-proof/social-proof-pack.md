## Story 1: Heroshe. Remittance with proof

**For:** HR managers and HR operations leads, especially in logistics.

**Before.** Remitting statutory deductions was the biggest HR problem Michelle had faced in years. It took "two weeks or more" to get processed, and the tax office kept calling. Most HR software, she says, could not help with statutory deductions at all.

**After.** The first time she remitted through Pade, "in less than a click" the payment had gone, with a summary of everything due. She gets a receipt for each remittance, and a call confirmed the payments were processed and remitted, "I think the next two days". Staff now see their own payslips, so they stop asking her for them, and she spends her time on strategic work.

**The quote to lead with (H2):**
> "With Pade, you're able to get your statutory deductions remitted and you also get a receipt once you're able to remit your payment, which just gives you an assurance that you've not just made payment, but you've gotten an evidence for the payment you've made."
> Michelle Adaware, Heroshe

**Supporting quotes:**
- Before (H3): "It's always a pain in the head because you have to wait for two weeks or more to get that processed."
- Before (H4): "Sometimes you keep getting calls from the tax office and honestly, it could be really overwhelming."
- After (H5): "It was so fast because in less than a click, I noticed that the payment had gone."
- Payslips (H7): "Once they get their payment, they see their payslip and that burden just goes off my neck."
- Strategic time (H8): "I leave the software to do the operational tasks and I do the strategic tasks."
- Peer recommendation (H9): "I would recommend Pade to any HR professional."

**Do not say:** "receipts within two days" (wrong: the two days refers to a confirmation call, and she hedges it with "I think"). Do not call Heroshe "100% compliant" from the video; that line is only on the written case study page, which also has unfinished placeholder copy.

## Story 2: FAMASI. The payroll hour back

**For:** founders and CEOs of growing teams who still run payroll themselves.

**Before.** Everything was manual. Adeola made each remittance by hand, checked each one had gone through, and texted every staff member to confirm they had been paid. She set aside about an hour every time she ran payroll. "It was stressful."

**Why she switched.** "As the team grew, we realized there was a need for a more sustainable HR solution." She tried a couple of other tools but they did not seem simple to use. Pade did, so she tried it.

**After.** That hour is saved, and she knows her people are paid. PaidHR reminds her before payroll day, and reports and remittances are in one place. She has been recommending it to others.

**The quote to lead with (F6):**
> "With Pade, I have that one hour saved and I mean, in entrepreneurship, time is very important. So I have that one hour saved to be able to focus on other things while being rest assured that Pade has my people paid."
> Adeola Ayoola, CEO and co-founder, FAMASI

**Supporting quotes:**
- Before (F1): "Everything was manual, like you have to make remittances manually, have to manually confirm that remittances have been done, also have to individually text people to confirm that they have received their payments. It was stressful."
- Before, time (F3): "I had to earmark about one hour of the day ... just to manually make payments."
- Chose it over others (F5): "I checked out a couple of them, but it just didn't seem very simple to use. And then I stumbled on Pade and it looked very simple."
- Ease (F7): "I get a notification before it's payroll day to say, do you want to run your payroll? It's easy for me to see the reports. It's easy for me to make the remittances."
- Recommendation (F8, F9): "I would definitely recommend Pade to other people. I have been recommending already." / "I think that everyone should get on it."

**Do not say:** "saves an hour a week" or "an hour a month". She said one hour each time she ran payroll; how often she runs it is not stated.
