## Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (effective 1 January 2026)

- **Signing and effective date:** signed June 2025. "the personal and payroll-related provisions take effect from January 1, 2026."
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria (PaidHR blog, checked 2026-11-14)
- **Signing date:** 26 June 2025, per search-result summaries of law-firm coverage.
  Source: https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/nigeria/insights/2026/FAQs-on-payroll-taxes-and-employer-liability (checked 2026-11-14)
- **New PAYE bands:** "lower earners with an income of ₦800,000 or less annually now benefit from a zero per cent (0%) tax rate, while the highest bracket, applying to income over ₦50,000,000, is set at 25%."
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Middle bands (CONFLICTING):** sources disagree on the middle bands. PaySpace lists 15%, 18% and 21% tiers. Treat the official NRS table as authoritative.
  Sources: https://payspace.com/blog/nigeria-tax-reform-2026 and https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-payroll-professional/f/announcements/261138/nigeria-payroll-tax-reform-changes-effective-january-2026 (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Rent relief:** "This allowance replaces the previous Consolidated Relief Allowance (CRA), allowing individuals who pay rent to claim 20% of their annual rent as a deduction from their gross income, subject to a maximum annual cap of ₦500,000."
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Broader taxable income:** "things like rent allowances, company-provided accommodation, car benefits, bonuses, and even digital rewards can now fall under taxable income."
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Residency:** "Anyone spending 183 days or more in Nigeria ... may be deemed a Nigerian tax resident and taxed on worldwide income. That includes remote workers who get paid in foreign currencies".
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Outdated payroll systems:** DLA Piper reports that payroll systems still using the old bands are producing incorrect deductions (search-result summary).
  Source: https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/nigeria/insights/2026/FAQs-on-payroll-taxes-and-employer-liability (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Withholding tax:** "your company must deduct and remit taxes whenever you make payments to service providers, landlords, or even third-party consultants."
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Small-company tax:** the 0% company income tax threshold rises from ₦25 million to ₦100 million turnover. A 4% Development Levy applies to large companies.
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria (checked 2026-11-14)

## New tax authority and digital enforcement

- **FIRS becomes NRS:** "The Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) is being transformed into the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS), a more tech-driven body with wider powers."
- **Data access:** "The NRS will have better access to banking, payroll, and transaction data, meaning fewer chances to 'forget' a remittance."
- **Penalties:** "There will be stricter penalties for late or inaccurate filings, especially for companies that fail to remit PAYE or Withholding Tax on time."

Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria (checked 2026-11-14)

## Filing deadlines and penalties

- **Monthly PAYE:** due by the 10th of the following month.
- **Annual PAYE return:** due by 31 January.
- **Penalty for missing the annual return:** ₦100,000 for the first month and ₦50,000 for each month after (search-result summaries).
- **PAYE is a state tax:** it is remitted to the State Internal Revenue Service where the employee lives.

Sources: https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/tax-authorities/1691640/know-your-taxes-the-tax-reforms-and-your-statutory-filing-obligations-a-simplified-guide, https://eirs.gov.ng/notice-of-demand-for-employers-employees-to-file-annual-paye-individual-tax-returns/ and https://arbiterz.com/?p=62589 (checked 2026-11-14)

- **State deadline extension:** Edo State extended the annual PAYE filing deadline to 14 February 2026.
  Source: https://gpa.net/blogs/africa/nigeria-edo-state-paye-filing-deadline-extended-to-february-14 (checked 2026-11-14)
- **INFERRED:** the next annual PAYE return deadline is 31 January 2027, covering 2026, the first year under the new bands.

## Pension enforcement (PenCom, 2026)

- **Lagos enforcement:** PenCom and the NLC Lagos Council planned joint enforcement in Lagos from June 2026, after complaints that employers deducted pension contributions but did not remit them.
  Source: https://nairametrics.com/2026/05/19/pencom-nlc-to-begin-enforcement-against-pension-remittance-defaulters-in-lagos/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Automated penalties:** PenCom announced automated penalty calculation against defaulters, after ₦36.6 billion in cumulative recoveries.
  Source: https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/14/pencom-targets-defaulting-employers-with-automated-crackdown-after-n36-6-billion-recoveries/ (2026-10-14, checked 2026-11-14)
- **Electricity sector recoveries:** PenCom and the ICPC recovered over ₦3 billion from electricity sector employers.
  Source: https://nairametrics.com/2026/07/01/pencom-icpc-recover-n3-billion-in-unremitted-pension-contributions-from-electricity-sector-employers/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Remittance timing:** employers must remit pension within seven working days of paying salaries (search-result summary).
  Source: https://businessday.ng/news/article/pension-remittance-pencom-tells-employers-to-comply-or-face-sanctions/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Criminal offence proposal:** PenCom's Director-General said: "Non-remittance of pensions should frankly be a criminal offence, and we are looking at how to reflect that in the new Pension Reform Act". Its status as law: Not found.
  Source: search-result summary of Leadership coverage, https://leadership.ng/employers-risk-blacklist-from-over-deductions-non-remittances-pencom/ (checked 2026-11-14)
