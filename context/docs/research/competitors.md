# PaidHR: Competitors

Raw research, not conclusions. All sources checked 2026-11-14 unless a different date is given.
Quotes are exact. "INFERRED" marks my interpretation. "Not found" means I looked and found nothing.

## SeamlessHR (Nigeria-headquartered, pan-African)

- **Headline:** "AI-powered HR Management Platform."
- **Subheadline:** "Streamline HR with AI-powered software built for office-based and shift-based teams"
- **Taglines:** "Run and finance payroll effortlessly", "The complete platform for shift-based teams", "Every worker, one single source of truth."
- **AI assistant:** "Samira".
- **Markets:** Kenya, Uganda, Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa. Payroll financing is in Nigeria and Kenya only.
- **Security claims:** ISO 27001 certified, GDPR compliant.
- **Named customers:** SanlamAllianz Nigeria, Halogen Security, Ampersand Energy, Sterling Bank, Konga Group, Venture Garden Group, Churchgate Group, Letshego Africa, Grant Thornton, AfyaCare.

Source: https://seamlesshr.com (checked 2026-11-14)

- **Innovation Village positioning:** "remains one of the most robust HR and payroll platforms built for African businesses", for "Growing SMEs and mid-sized companies".
  Source: https://innovation-village.com/top-7-payroll-software-solutions-for-nigerian-smes-a-2026-comparison/ (2026-05-04, checked 2026-11-14)
- **Content:** SeamlessHR publishes "Top HR software in Nigeria 2026" lists ranking itself first, plus tax-reform payroll guides.
  Source: https://seamlesshr.com/blog/top-hr-management-software-in-nigeria-in-2026/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **INFERRED:** SeamlessHR targets larger, enterprise and shift-based employers (banks, security, retail groups), more upmarket than PaidHR's named logos.

## Workpay (Kenya-headquartered, pan-African)

- **Headline:** "The EOR and Payroll Solution for Africa"
- **Subheadline:** "Payroll, EOR, and Outsourced Payroll Solutions for growing companies in Africa, helping you expand regionally, hire without entities, and stay compliant."
- **Tagline:** "Simple. Compliant. Scalable."
- **Claims:** "54 Countries", "1,000+ Companies", "ISO 27001" (the same page also says "over 400 companies").
- **Modules:** EOR, Outsourced Payroll, Payroll and Self Service HR, Financial Services, Time and Attendance, Perks and Benefits, Expense Claims, Leaves. Performance Management is marked "Coming soon".
- **Named customers:** Flutterwave, Komaza, MyDawa, CIS Kenya, Yoco, Marketforce, Plan International and others.

Source: https://www.myworkpay.com (checked 2026-11-14)

- **Funding:** $5M Series A led by Norrsken22, with Visa and Y Combinator participating.
  Source: https://techeconomy.ng/workpay-secures-5m-series-a-funding-to-expand-hr-and-payroll-solutions-across-africa/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **INFERRED:** Workpay leads with EOR and multi-country hiring. PaidHR's cross-border payroll overlaps, but PaidHR has no EOR claim on its site.

## SoftSUITE (Nigeria)

- **Headline:** "Nigeria's Most Compliant HR & Payroll Platform"
- **Subheadline:** "SoftSUITE is built specifically for Nigerian businesses — from growing SMEs to public sector institutions."
- **Tagline:** "Run Payroll. Manage People. Stay Compliant."
- **Claims:** "500+" Companies, "2M+" Employees Managed, "₦2B+" Payroll processed monthly, "99%" Compliance accuracy.
- **Segments:** SMEs, Large Organisations, Government & MDAs, Banks & Fintechs.

Source: https://www.softsuite.com (checked 2026-11-14)

- **INFERRED:** SoftSUITE competes head-on with PaidHR's "compliance" message in Nigeria, and also sells to the public sector.

## Bento Africa (Nigeria, operations paused)

- **Status:** paused operations in February 2025 after its CEO resigned. The board told clients to stop funding payroll accounts.
  Sources: https://techcabal.com/2025/02/10/bento-africa-temporarily-shut-down/ and https://techeconomy.ng/bento-africa-temporarily-shuts-down-following-ceo-exit (search results, checked 2026-11-14)
- **Allegations:** reported claims that Bento failed to remit taxes and pensions, and forged tax receipts. The Lagos State Internal Revenue Service reportedly investigated.
  Source: search-result summaries of the above coverage (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Scale at its peak:** "over 1,000 businesses, including Moniepoint, Paystack, and Bamboo" (search-result summary of a 2025 shutdown roundup).
  Source: https://techpoint.africa/insight/african-startups-shutdown-2025/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Still listed in 2026:** Innovation Village's May 2026 roundup still lists Bento, for "SMEs focused on employee welfare and retention".
  Source: https://innovation-village.com/top-7-payroll-software-solutions-for-nigerian-smes-a-2026-comparison/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Status after 2025:** Not found.

## How PaidHR positions against them (its own words)

- **Integration:** "Disconnected tools cost more than money."
- **One platform:** "People operations without the patchwork"
- **Compliance:** "Zero-default compliance"
- **Tax law:** "Tax-law ready: Fully updated for Nigeria's new tax law, effective 1 January 2026 — before your first affected payroll run."

Source: https://www.paidhr.com/ (checked 2026-11-14)

- **INFERRED:** PaidHR's distinct angle against these competitors is employee financial wellness, bundled at no cost to the employer: EWA, the Loan Marketplace and the wallet, plus WhatsApp and USSD access. Of the above, SeamlessHR (payroll financing) and Workpay (financial services, EWA) also claim parts of this.
