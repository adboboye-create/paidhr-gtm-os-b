# PaidHR: Ghana statutory filings and PaidHR Ghana coverage

Raw research, not conclusions. All sources checked 2026-11-14.
"Official" = GRA, SSNIT or NPRA. "Secondary" = vendor or blog guides.

## Part 1: What a Ghanaian employer must file each month

| Item | Paid to | Deadline | Rate | Source quality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PAYE (income tax) | Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) | On or before the 15th of the following month | Individual income tax bands | Official (page is old; re-check bands) |
| SSNIT Tier 1 | SSNIT | Within 14 days after month end (the 14th) | 13.5% of basic salary (from the 18.5% total) | Official |
| Tier 2 occupational pension | Employee's private Tier 2 trustee (regulated by NPRA) | Within 14 days after month end | 5% of basic salary | Official (old NPRA guideline) |
| Total social security | SSNIT + Tier 2 | as above | 18.5% of basic: 5.5% employee + 13% employer | Official |

### Quotes and sources
- **PAYE:** "Monthly PAYE returns must be filed by the employer on behalf of the employee on or before the fifteenth day of the month following the month in which the deduction was made." Source: https://gra.gov.gh/domestic-tax/tax-types/paye/
- **SSNIT split:** "Deduct 5.5% from the worker's monthly salary and add 13% of their basic salary to total an 18.5% contribution." Source: https://www.ssnit.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/SSNIT-Omnibus.pdf
- **Tier 1 deadline:** employer "remits 13.5% to SSNIT within 14 days following the end of the month". Source: https://www.ssnit.org.gh/faqs/
- **Tier 2:** "An employer shall within fourteen (14) days from the end of each month transfer the five per centum (5%) contributions ... to the second tier mandatory occupational scheme". Source: https://npra.gov.gh/assets/documents/Guidelines-for-the-payment-of-contributions.pdf
- **Not verified:** the SSNIT salary cap (FAQ mentions GH¢25,000; may be outdated). Do not use.
- **INFERRED:** a Ghanaian employer pays three bodies (GRA, SSNIT, a Tier 2 trustee) on two deadlines (14th and 15th).
