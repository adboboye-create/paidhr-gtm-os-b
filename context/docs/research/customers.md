## Employee app reviews (Google Play, "Pade" app)

Source for everything below: [raw/treg-googleplay-reviews-pade-2026-11-14.json](raw/treg-googleplay-reviews-pade-2026-11-14.json), via treg, `litescrape.google_play.reviews`, app `com.pade.mobile.pade`, Nigeria store (checked 2026-11-14).

- **Volume:** 17 reviews returned, from 2024-01-18 to 2026-08-13.
- **INFERRED:** the average of the returned ratings is 3.5 out of 5. I computed this from the 17 reviews; it is not Google's official score.
- **Positive quotes:**
  - "This app makes doing every action like checking salary payment and making withdrawals easy" (5 stars, 2026-08-13)
  - "very simple and accurate" (5 stars, 2025-06-29)
  - "It is great and very easy to navigate" (5 stars, 2025-05-21)
  - "Seamless transactions" (5 stars, 2025-12-10)
- **Negative quotes:**
  - "The app keeps logging out despite having the latest update." (1 star, 2025-07-11)
  - "can't login. I keep being redirected to the login page after every login attempt. quite frustrating..." (1 star, 2025-06-21)
  - "The app keeps telling me to update after I've updated on the play store" (1 star, 2025-07-11)
  - "Keep getting string is not a subtype of null 😂. Developers why na 😂" (2 stars, 2026-01-16)
  - "No where to sign up why" (1 star, 2024-01-18)
- **INFERRED:** the reviewer behind the 2026-08-13 five-star review has the same name as the author of PaidHR's WhatsApp and USSD blog post (https://www.paidhr.com/blog/using-paidhr-on-whatsapp-ussd), so it may be an employee review.
