# PaidHR: Customer videos

Raw research, not conclusions. All videos checked 2026-11-14.

## How these were made

- **Videos:** found by reading the embedded YouTube links on https://www.paidhr.com/customers.
- **Transcripts:** YouTube's auto-generated English captions, downloaded with yt-dlp. No one checked them by hand, so they contain speech-recognition errors. Raw timestamped transcripts are in [raw/](raw/).
- **Quotes:** given exactly as captioned. Where the captions plainly misheard a name, my correction is in [square brackets] and is INFERRED. The usual mishearings are "paid" for "Pade" (PaidHR's old name), "Pharmacy" for "FAMASI" and "Hiroshi" for "Heroshe".
- **Meaning:** the captions don't punctuate sentences or mark who is speaking, so each quote's meaning is clear but its wording is approximate.

The customers page embeds three unique videos (one appears twice). Two are customer stories; one is a product video.

## 1. FAMASI

Source: https://youtu.be/thPhRuim05g (checked 2026-11-14)

- **Speaker:** the YouTube description says "Adeola Ayoola's conversation over at FAMASI". The captions read "hi my name is Adele [Adeola] I'm the CEO and co-founder at Pharmacy [FAMASI]".
- **Note:** FAMASI is not on PaidHR's case-study page or customer sitemap ([customers.md](customers.md)), so this video is the only public source naming it as a customer.

### Facts
- **What FAMASI does:** "we're building the OS for pharmacies in Emerging Markets by building software and apis that allow businesses and individuals connect to pharmacists"
- **Before Pade:** remittances were made and confirmed by hand, and the CEO texted each employee to confirm they were paid.
- **Why they switched:** "as a team [grew] we realized there was a need for a more sustainable HR solution". The captions say "as a team girl"; "grew" is INFERRED.
- **Alternatives:** she tried others first: "I checked out a couple of them but it just didn't seem very simple to use"
- **Features named:** a reminder before payroll, reports, and remittances.

### Numbers
- **One hour per payroll run:** "I had to [earmark] about one hour of the day whenever I [sat down] to run payroll just to manually make payments". The captions say "air Mark" and "signed"; the corrections are INFERRED.
- **Time saved:** "[with] paid [Pade] I have that one hour [saved]". The captions say "received"; "saved" is INFERRED.

### Quotes
- [00:00:00] "everything was manual like you have to make remittances manually have to manually confirm that remittances have been done also have to individually text people to confirm that they have received their payments was stressful"
- [00:00:41] "I stumbled on paid [Pade] and it looked very simple so I decided to give it a try"
- [00:01:00] "in entrepreneurship time is very important"
- [00:01:00] "being rest assured that paid [Pade] has my people paid"
- [00:01:00] "I would definitely recommend paint [Pade] to other people I have been recommending already I feel like I have like referral bonuses stacked up already"
- [00:01:20] "it has made my life simpler I get a notification before it's payroll date to say do you want to run your payroll it's easy for me to see the reports it's easy for me to make the remittances it's just a very simple interface to use"
- [00:01:20] "I'm particularly excited for the solutions that make it easier for my team to enjoy the [money] that they do and access other services". The captions say "one that they do". INFERRED: this refers to the employee financial features that later became EWA.

## 2. Heroshe

Source: https://youtu.be/1L3R7CMEmjI (checked 2026-11-14)

- **Speaker:** the YouTube description says "Michelle Adaware's conversation over at Heroshe". The captions read "my name is Michelle Adare I'm T4 operations specialist here at Hiroshi [Heroshe]". Her job title is unclear in the captions: "T4 operations specialist". INFERRED: possibly "HR operations specialist", not confirmed.
- **Naming mismatch:** the written Heroshe case study on PaidHR's site quotes an "Adewunmi", not Michelle (see [customers.md](customers.md)).

### Facts
- **What Heroshe does:** "a service oriented business that deals with cross-border shipping". Products come from the US and UK to Nigeria and Ghana. The captions say "from us and UK dance Nigeria and Ghana"; INFERRED reading: "from the US and UK to Nigeria and Ghana".
- **Main pain:** remitting statutory deductions (tax and pension): slow processing and calls from the tax office.
- **What changed:** remittance was fast, she could see a summary of all payments, and receipts arrived afterwards as evidence.
- **Fewer employee questions:** employees see their own payslips, so they no longer ask her.
- **Features named:** statutory remittance with receipts, a payment summary, employee payslips, anniversary notifications.

### Numbers
- **Before:** "you have to wait for two weeks or more to get that processed"
- **After:** receipts came "in I think the next two days"
- **Speed:** "in less than a click I noticed that the payments had gone"

### Quotes
- [00:00:00] "what I've noticed about most HR software is the fact that they're unable to help most HR professionals process [statutory] deductions which has been a pain to most of us". The captions say "start to treat"; "statutory" is INFERRED.
- [00:00:00] "with paid [Pade] you're able to get your [statutory] deductions remitted and you also gets a receipts once you're able to remit your payment which just gives you an assurance that you've not just made payment but you've gotten an evidence for the payment you've made". The captions say "surgery"; "statutory" is INFERRED.
- [00:00:42] "the major HR problem or challenge I faced in a couple of years will be remitting statutory deductions it's always a pain in the head because you have to wait for two weeks or more to get that processed and sometimes you keep getting calls from the tax office and honestly could be really overwhelming"
- [00:00:42] "I remember the first time when I used paid [Pade] to remit my salary deductions it was so fast because in less than a click I noticed that the payments had gone"
- [00:01:25] "once they get their payments they see their payslip and that [burden] just goes off my neck". The captions say "body"; "burden" is INFERRED.
- [00:01:43] "I leave the software to do the operational [tasks] and I do the Strategic task". The captions say "attacks"; "tasks" is INFERRED.
- [00:01:43] "I'll recommend pay [Pade] to any HR professional if you're a young HR professional or even if you're mid-level experience"
