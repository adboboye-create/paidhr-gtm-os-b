## Observations (INFERRED)

- **Two audiences:** the voice speaks to the employer ("Control for the business") and to the employee ("Wellbeing for the people") in the same breath.
- **"Zero" as a device:** "Zero" is a repeated claim: "Zero cost", "Zero financial risk", "Zero disruption", "Zero defaults".
- **Brand slips:** older pages still say "Pade" and "N" for naira; newer pages use "₦". There are typos on live pages ("toady", "qoute", "whrere", "caccess").
