# PaidHR: Buying signals (public evidence)

Raw research, not conclusions. All sources checked 2026-11-14 unless a different date is given.
Each signal lists the evidence that it matters. The step from evidence to "this company needs PaidHR now" is always INFERRED.

## 1. New tax law: first year of new PAYE bands

- **Evidence:** the payroll provisions of the Nigeria Tax Act took effect on 1 January 2026.
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Evidence:** DLA Piper reports payroll systems on the old bands are producing incorrect deductions.
  Source: https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/nigeria/insights/2026/FAQs-on-payroll-taxes-and-employer-liability (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Evidence:** PaidHR's own claim: "Fully updated for Nigeria's new tax law, effective 1 January 2026".
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **INFERRED trigger:** the company runs payroll on spreadsheets or a tool that hasn't updated for the new bands, rent relief or the wider definition of taxable benefits.
- **INFERRED timing:** the first annual PAYE return under the new law is due 31 January 2027.

## 2. Pension enforcement in Lagos

- **Evidence:** PenCom and the NLC began joint enforcement in Lagos from June 2026.
  Source: https://nairametrics.com/2026/05/19/pencom-nlc-to-begin-enforcement-against-pension-remittance-defaulters-in-lagos/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Evidence:** PenCom announced automated penalties after ₦36.6bn in recoveries.
  Source: https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/14/pencom-targets-defaulting-employers-with-automated-crackdown-after-n36-6-billion-recoveries/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **INFERRED trigger:** a Lagos employer in a sector being targeted (electricity has already been hit) that remits pension manually.

## 3. Displaced or distrustful payroll-outsourcing customers

- **Evidence:** Bento Africa paused operations in February 2025 and told clients to stop funding payroll. At its peak it reportedly served over 1,000 businesses.
  Sources: https://techcabal.com/2025/02/10/bento-africa-temporarily-shut-down/ and https://techpoint.africa/insight/african-startups-shutdown-2025/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Evidence:** one reported client switched to manual payments while it looked for a new provider (search-result summary).
  Source: Bento coverage above (checked 2026-11-14)
- **INFERRED trigger:** a company that used Bento or another outsourced payroll provider and now wants proof that remittance happens. PaidHR leads with "Zero" "Statutory defaults or delays".
- **INFERRED caveat:** this signal is 21 months old as of 2026-11-14, so most of those companies have probably moved already.

## 4. Fresh funding

- **Evidence:** Nigerian startups raised about $635M from January to September 2026.
  Source: https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/21/nigeria-reclaims-africas-startup-funding-lead-as-investments-rise-to-635-million/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Evidence:** PaidHR's named customers include many funded startups, such as Flutterwave, Mono, Risevest and GetEquity.
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/case-study (checked 2026-11-14)
- **INFERRED trigger:** a newly funded company crossing 20 employees, which is the Standard tier floor (https://www.paidhr.com/pricing).

## 5. Headcount crossing 20, or growing past 100

- **Evidence:** pricing tiers are "Standard" for "20 - 100 Employees" and "Enterprise" for "100+".
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/pricing (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Evidence:** "The majority of PaidHR's users are logistics, retail, and manufacturing businesses with under 200 employees" (exact quote, re-checked 2026-11-14).
  Source: https://www.accion.org/news/paidhr-raises-1-8m-seed-round-led-by-accion-venture-lab-to-boost-africas-growing-workforce/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **INFERRED trigger:** headcount growth into 20 to 200, especially in logistics, retail or manufacturing.

## 6. Hourly, shift or frontline workforce

- **Evidence:** "Manage clock-ins, shifts and overtime with geo-referenced tracking".
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Evidence:** "On USSD, you can withdraw earned wages, pay bills ... from any phone, with no internet connection needed."
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/using-paidhr-on-whatsapp-ussd (checked 2026-11-14)
- **INFERRED trigger:** an employer with many low-wage or field staff and no smartphone or data access, where EWA and USSD fit.

## 7. Employee financial stress and retention problems

- **Evidence:** "N400m+ Total EWA disbursed", "1500+ Active users".
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/earned-wage-access-pade/employers (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Evidence:** the CEO on EWA: "we crossed ₦50 million in a single month."
  Source: https://www.raenest.com/es-ar/blog/behind-the-growth-how-paidhr-found-the-right-problem-to-solve (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Evidence:** PaidHR's copy: "Unclear pay & salaries that vanish before month-end make people leave."
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **INFERRED trigger:** high staff turnover, salary-advance requests handled by hand, or job posts that mention "salary advance" as a benefit.

## 8. Cross-border or foreign-currency staff

- **Evidence:** "$2M+ in cross-border payments processed since October 2024".
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/ (checked 2026-11-14)
- **Evidence:** remote workers living in Nigeria and paid in foreign currency may be tax residents.
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria (checked 2026-11-14)
- **INFERRED trigger:** a company hiring outside Nigeria (Kenya, South Africa) or paying staff in USD or GBP.

## 9. New HR or finance leader

- **Evidence:** Not found. I found no public data on PaidHR deals linked to new HR hires.
- **INFERRED trigger only:** a new Head of HR, HR Manager or Finance Manager often reviews tools in their first 90 days. This needs job-change data (for example LinkedIn) to test.

## 10. Hiring for HR or payroll roles

- **Evidence:** Not found. The treg job-postings endpoint (`mobiusengine-jobs.jobs.company`) needs a separate provider credential and was not run. No charge.
- **INFERRED trigger only:** a company posting for a "Payroll Officer" or "HR Generalist" is building payroll in-house and might buy software instead.
