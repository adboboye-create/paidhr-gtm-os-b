# PaidHR messaging

Sources in [brackets] are files in `context/docs/research/`. Every number used in copy must come from `facts.md`. Last updated 2026-11-14.

## Core message

PaidHR runs payroll and files your statutory remittances on time, with receipts, and gives your staff access to their earned pay at no cost to you.

Sources: [company.md], [customer-videos.md]

## Pillars

| Pillar | Message | Proof points | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Compliance with proof | PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF filed automatically, receipts after each remittance | ₦934M PAYE and ₦754M pension processed in 2025 (own claim); Heroshe: receipts within about two days | [company.md], [customer-videos.md] |
| 2. Payroll without the manual work | Payroll on any schedule; staff see their own payslips | FAMASI CEO saved about one hour per payroll run; ₦119.2bn payroll processed in 2026 YTD (own claim) | [customer-videos.md], [company.md] |
| 3. Financial wellbeing at no cost to the employer | EWA and Loan Marketplace; PaidHR or the lender carries the risk | EWA N400m+ disbursed, 1500+ active users; Yield Bank partner | [company.md] |
| 4. Ready for the new tax law | Updated for the Nigeria Tax Act, effective 1 January 2026 | Own claim on homepage | [voice.md], [market.md] |

## Words to use
- remittance, receipts, on time, payslip, simple, earned wages, PAYE, pension, NHF, NSITF (PaidHR's and customers' own words) [voice.md], [customer-videos.md]
- "Pade" only when quoting the 2023 videos; otherwise "PaidHR" [voice.md]

## Words and angles to avoid
- Global bans in `knowledge-base/playbooks/copy-bans.yaml`, including "seamless", "streamline", "empower", "robust", "free", "guarantee"
- "loan" when describing EWA ("EWA isn't a loan or a gamble") [voice.md]
- "#1": PaidHR uses it in a page title but research has no proof [company.md]
- Naming Bento or implying a competitor committed fraud [competitors.md]
- Conflicting numbers: customer count, employee count, currencies, countries (see `facts.md`) [company.md]
- Em dashes, en dashes, exclamation marks
