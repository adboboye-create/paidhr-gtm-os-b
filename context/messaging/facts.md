## Banned

### Banned angles
- Naming Bento Africa or implying any competitor committed fraud. (competitors.md)
- Calling EWA a loan. (voice.md)
- "#1" or "most compliant" claims. No proof in research. (company.md, competitors.md)
- EOR or "hire without an entity". PaidHR makes no such claim. (competitors.md)
- Guarantees of outcomes.
- Anything that diagnoses the prospect's team as failing (iron law 14 + the insult test).

### Banned words and phrasing (company-specific)
Global list: `knowledge-base/playbooks/copy-bans.yaml`. PaidHR-specific:
- "Pade" (legacy name) outside a quote
- "N" for naira (use "₦")
- "loan" for EWA

- The Heroshe placeholder quote. (customers.md)
