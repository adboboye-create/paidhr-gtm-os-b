## Where PaidHR wins
- **Compliance with proof:** remittance receipts; "₦934M in PAYE filed and ₦754M in pension processed in 2025" (own claim). [company.md], [customer-videos.md]
- **Simplicity:** the FAMASI CEO tried others that "didn't seem very simple to use". [customer-videos.md]
- **Employee money features at zero employer cost:** EWA, Loan Marketplace, WhatsApp and USSD. [company.md]
- **Tax-law readiness:** "Fully updated for Nigeria's new tax law, effective 1 January 2026". [voice.md]

## Where PaidHR loses
- **EOR and wide multi-country coverage:** Workpay. [competitors.md]
- **Enterprise logos and proof volume:** SeamlessHR names banks and groups; PaidHR has 2 customer stories. [competitors.md], [customers.md]
- **Funding and scale:** Workpay $5M Series A vs PaidHR $1.8M seed. [competitors.md], [company.md]
- **Public sector:** SoftSUITE. [competitors.md]
- **Employee app ratings:** about 3.5 from 17 Play Store reviews (INFERRED average). [customers.md]
