## Positioning statement

For growing Nigerian businesses that still run payroll and statutory remittances by hand, PaidHR is the HR and payroll platform that pays staff on time and files PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF automatically, with receipts as proof. Unlike HR tools that stop at payroll, PaidHR also gives employees access to earned wages and credit at no cost to the employer, from an app, WhatsApp or USSD.
