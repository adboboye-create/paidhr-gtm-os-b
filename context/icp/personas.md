# PaidHR buyer personas

Sources in [brackets] are files in `context/docs/research/`. Quotes from videos are auto-captions with corrections in [square brackets]; check against the video before using in copy. Last updated 2026-11-14.

## 1. Champion: HR manager / HR operations

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | HR Manager; HR operations specialist (title unclear in captions); "HR/Finance admin" | [customers.md], [customer-videos.md], [company.md] |
| Measured on (evidence) | Statutory deductions remitted on time with proof; fewer staff pay queries; time for strategic work | [customer-videos.md] Heroshe |
| What makes them look | Slow remittance, calls from the tax office, new tax rules | [customer-videos.md], [market.md] |

Top pains, own words (Heroshe, [customer-videos.md]):
1. "remitting statutory deductions it's always a pain in the head because you have to wait for two weeks or more to get that processed"
2. "sometimes you keep getting calls from the tax office and honestly could be really overwhelming"
3. "most HR software is the fact that they're unable to help most HR professionals process [statutory] deductions"

Objections:
- Trust that remittance actually happens (Bento context). Proof: receipts after each remittance. [competitors.md], [customer-videos.md]
- Employee app reliability: Play Store reviews cite login loops and forced updates. [customers.md]
- Open question: implementation effort. Research only says PaidHR offers "in-depth implementation training". Not in research files beyond that.

## 2. Economic buyer: founder / CEO (SME)

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | CEO and co-founder; founders and business operators | [customer-videos.md] FAMASI, [signals.md] Moonshot post |
| Measured on (evidence) | Founder time; people paid on time; company compliant | [customer-videos.md] FAMASI |
| What makes them look | Team growth; time lost to manual payroll | [customer-videos.md] |

Top pains, own words (FAMASI CEO, [customer-videos.md]):
1. "everything was manual like you have to make remittances manually have to manually confirm that remittances have been done"
2. "I had to [earmark] about one hour of the day whenever I [sat down] to run payroll just to manually make payments"
3. "have to individually text people to confirm that they have received their payments was stressful"

Objections:
- Ease of use: "I checked out a couple of them but it just didn't seem very simple to use". [customer-videos.md]
- EWA risk: PaidHR addresses "It might seem risky to employers at first". [voice.md]
- Open question: price. No prices are published. [company.md]
