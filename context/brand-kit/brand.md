# PaidHR brand: tone and key phrases

All from PaidHR's own copy in `context/docs/research/voice.md` unless noted. Last updated 2026-11-14.

## Tone of voice

| Trait | Example from PaidHR's copy |
|---|---|
| Speaks to employer and employee at once | "Control for the business. Wellbeing for the people." |
| Reassuring about risk | "EWA isn't a loan or a gamble; it's simply giving employees access to money they've already earned so it adds no risk to your business." |
| "Zero" as a device | "Zero cost", "Zero financial risk", "Zero disruption", "Zero defaults" |
| Empathy for employees | "You shouldn't empty your paycheck just to survive till payday." |
| Playful, Nigerian English on social | "\"Wow! Your software is incredibleeee!\" Chakam! 📸" |
| Short, punchy lines | "No app switching. No internet? No problem." |

## Key phrases

- Tagline: "Work Made Simple, Lives Made Better"
- Mission: "Making work simple and life better, for everyone."
- "Better workplaces run on PaidHR."
- "Disconnected tools cost more than money."
- "People operations without the patchwork"
- "That's retention you don't have to buy."
- "Support your team without spending a naira."
- "Your salary, your timeline."
- "One platform. Multiple solutions. A better way to work."

## Product names

PaidHR, PaidHiring, Cross Border by PaidHR, Earned Wage Access (#EWAbyPaidHR), PaidHR Loan Marketplace, PaidHR Employee Wallet, PaidGuide, PaidRight, Pade (employee app, legacy name).

## Signature habits

- Social posts end with "Book a demo today!" and the blue heart 💙.
- Hashtags: #GETPaid, #EWAbyPaidHR.
- CTAs: "Book a Demo", "Get Started", "Talk to a Product Specialist".

## Watch-outs (from research)

- Live pages still say "Pade" and "N" for naira; newer pages use "₦". Use "PaidHR" and "₦".
- Typos on live pages ("toady", "qoute", "whrere", "caccess"). Don't copy them.
- PaidHR's own copy uses exclamation marks and em dashes; our outbound copy does not (`copy-bans.yaml`).
